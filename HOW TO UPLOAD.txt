DBS HEALTH & FITNESS: upload steps

BEFORE YOU START (keeps your data)
1. Open the current app on your iPhone > Settings > Your data > Export file. Save it to Files / iCloud Drive.

CLEAR GITHUB
2. github.com/dbs86/training > Settings > scroll to bottom > Delete this repository.
3. Create a new public repository called "training" (no README).

UPLOAD
4. On the empty repo page click "uploading an existing file".
5. Open this folder in Finder, select everything inside it (Cmd+A, including the icons and splash folders) and drag it in. Commit.
   Hidden file .nojekyll: press Cmd+Shift+. in Finder to show it, so it gets dragged in too.
6. Settings > Pages > Source: Deploy from a branch > main / (root) > Save. Wait 1-2 minutes.

ON YOUR IPHONE
7. Delete the old DBS PPLX icon from your Home Screen.
8. In Safari open https://dbs86.github.io/training/
9. Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
10. Open the new icon > Settings > Your data > Import file > pick the backup from step 1.

SPOTIFY (only if you use it)
The app address is now https://dbs86.github.io/training/ so update the Redirect URI in your Spotify developer dashboard to match.

FILES
index.html            the app
manifest.json         app name and icons
sw.js                 offline support
app.html              forwards old links to the new address
icons/                home screen icon and favicons
splash/               launch screens for each iPhone size
